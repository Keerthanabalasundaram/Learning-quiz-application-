 # 📚 LEARNING QUIZ APP

---

## 1. PROJECT TITLE

📌 LEARNING QUIZ APP

---

## 2. PROBLEM STATEMENT

Students often struggle with **EFFECTIVE REVISION** and **EXAM PREPARATION**. Traditional quizzes are static, time-consuming to create, and fail to adapt to individual learner performance.  

There is also a lack of engaging, gamified platforms that combine **PERSONALIZED QUESTIONS**, **STRUCTURED PROGRESSION**, **ADAPTIVE DIFFICULTY**, and **MOTIVATIONAL FEEDBACK**.  

The **LEARNING QUIZ APP** aims to solve these challenges by providing an **ADAPTIVE, LEVEL-BASED QUIZ SYSTEM** that generates MCQs from study material and offers **INBUILT UNIT-WISE QUESTION BANKS**, integrated with **TIMERS**, **ANALYTICS**, and **MOTIVATIONAL FEEDBACK**.

---

## 3. PROJECT OBJECTIVES

- ✅ **AUTOMATIC MCQ GENERATION:** Generate multiple-choice questions from uploaded study material.  
- ✅ **INBUILT QUESTION BANK:** Preloaded, unit-wise, and subject-wise questions for immediate use.  
- ✅ **ADAPTIVE DIFFICULTY:** Harder questions after correct answers; easier ones after incorrect.  
- ✅ **LEVEL-BASED PROGRESSION:** Unlock units sequentially for structured learning.  
- ✅ **TIMERS:** Per-question and overall quiz session timers.  
- ✅ **MOTIVATIONAL FEEDBACK:** Congratulatory visuals for success and encouraging animations on failure.  
- ✅ **ANALYTICS DASHBOARD:** Track scores, accuracy, time spent, and unit-wise progress.  
- ✅ **CSV BULK UPLOAD:** Efficient question management for administrators.  
- ✅ **SECURE AUTHENTICATION:** JWT-based login and signup system.

---

## 4. MODULE LIST

- 🔹 **AUTHENTICATION MODULE:** Handles user login/signup with JWT security.  
- 🔹 **QUESTION GENERATION MODULE:** Generates MCQs automatically from uploaded material.  
- 🔹 **INBUILT QUESTION BANK MODULE:** Preloaded unit-wise and subject-wise questions.  
- 🔹 **QUIZ ENGINE MODULE:** Manages timers, adaptive difficulty, and level progression.  
- 🔹 **FEEDBACK & ANIMATION MODULE:** Provides motivational and congratulatory visuals.  
- 🔹 **ANALYTICS MODULE:** Tracks performance, identifies strengths/weaknesses, and unit-wise progress.  
- 🔹 **ADMIN MODULE:** Enables CSV bulk upload, question management, and result oversight.

---

## 5. TABLE LIST

- 🔹 **USERS TABLE:** Stores user details, authentication credentials, and roles.  
- 🔹 **QUESTIONS TABLE:** Contains AI-generated and inbuilt questions with difficulty and unit mapping.  
- 🔹 **QUIZZES TABLE:** Records quiz sessions, timers, and progression.  
- 🔹 **RESULTS TABLE:** Stores scores, accuracy, time taken, and feedback outcomes.  
- 🔹 **UNITS TABLE:** Maps questions to specific units for structured progression.  
- 🔹 **UPLOADS TABLE:** Tracks uploaded study material and CSV imports.

---

## 6. SUMMARY

The **LEARNING QUIZ APP** is a **GAMIFIED, ADAPTIVE PLATFORM** that transforms studying into an **ENGAGING JOURNEY**.  

By combining **AI-GENERATED QUESTIONS**, **INBUILT UNIT-WISE QUIZZES**, **ADAPTIVE DIFFICULTY**, **TIMERS**, **LEVELS**, and **MOTIVATIONAL FEEDBACK**, it delivers a **PERSONALIZED, EXAM-READY LEARNING EXPERIENCE**.
